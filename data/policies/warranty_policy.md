# NovaShop Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty

Eligible products include a one-year limited warranty against
manufacturing defects.

## Covered Problems

The warranty may cover defects caused by manufacturing or
materials.

## Not Covered

The warranty does not normally cover:

- accidental damage
- misuse
- unauthorized modification
- normal wear and tear

## Evidence

NovaShop may request photographs, purchase information or
additional details before approving a warranty claim.

## Resolution

Depending on the case, NovaShop may offer:

- repair
- replacement
- partial refund
- full refund

The appropriate resolution depends on the product condition,
purchase history and applicable policy.
