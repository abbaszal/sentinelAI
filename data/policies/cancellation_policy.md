# NovaShop Cancellation Policy

## Before Shipment

An order may normally be cancelled while it is still in
processing status and no shipment has been created.

## After Shipment

Once an order has been shipped, normal cancellation is no
longer available.

The customer may instead follow the return procedure after
delivery.

## Already Cancelled Orders

An order that is already cancelled must not be cancelled again.

## Payment After Cancellation

If an order was cancelled but a completed payment still exists,
the payment must be investigated.

The existence of a completed payment does not automatically
mean that a refund has already been issued.

Refund records must also be checked.

## High-Risk Actions

Actual cancellation actions should require confirmation before
execution.

An AI system may recommend or draft a cancellation, but should
not execute an irreversible cancellation automatically.
