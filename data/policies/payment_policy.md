# NovaShop Payment Policy

## Successful Payments

A payment with status "completed" is considered successfully
captured.

Payments with status "failed" are not considered charges.

## Duplicate Payments

A possible duplicate payment exists when multiple completed
payments are recorded for the same order and the total
completed payment amount exceeds the order total.

When a duplicate payment is confirmed, the excess amount
should normally be refunded.

## Failed Payments

A failed payment does not count as a successful charge.

The customer should not receive goods based only on a failed
payment.

## Payment Verification

Before concluding that a customer was charged twice,
NovaShop must inspect all payment records associated with
the order.

Payment records must not be judged only by the number of
transactions. Their status and amount must also be checked.

## Refund Destination

Refunds related to payment errors should normally be returned
to the original payment method.
