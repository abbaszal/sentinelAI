# NovaShop Shipping Policy

## Processing

Orders may remain in processing status before being handed
to the carrier.

An order in processing status does not yet have a confirmed
shipment.

## Shipped Orders

Once a shipment record exists and the shipment is in transit,
the order should normally be considered shipped.

## Delivery

An order marked delivered by the shipping provider is
considered delivered unless conflicting evidence exists.

## Tracking

Customers may request shipment status using their order
information.

Shipment information should be obtained from trusted
operational records or the shipping provider.

## Delays

A shipment that remains in transit longer than expected may
require further investigation.

The system should not claim that a package is lost without
sufficient evidence.

## Shipping Refunds

Shipping fees are normally not refunded except when allowed
by the refund policy, such as certain damaged or incorrect
shipments.
