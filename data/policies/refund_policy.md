# NovaShop Refund Policy

## Standard Return Window

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days
after the order is marked as delivered.

The returned item must normally be unused and in its
original condition.

## Damaged Items

If an item arrives damaged, the customer may request
a refund or replacement.

Photographic evidence may be requested for damaged items.

A damaged-item claim should normally be submitted within
14 days of delivery.

## Refund Amount

A full refund may include the product price.

Original shipping fees are normally not refundable unless
the shipment was incorrect or the item arrived damaged.

## Partial Refunds

NovaShop may issue a partial refund when:

- only part of an order is affected
- an item has minor damage
- a customer agrees to keep a damaged item

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.

Refund processing may take between 3 and 7 business days.

## Already Refunded Orders

The system must check previous refund records before proposing
another refund.

The total refunded amount must not exceed the amount that was
actually paid for the order.
